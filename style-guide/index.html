GEARS

One geometry. Four gears.

Every screen reduces to three Bauhaus primitives — the circle, the bar, the right angle — drawn from nine colour ramps. Each of the four gears carries one colour as its signature.

00

Principles

Three rules the whole system answers to. When in doubt, subtract.

Primary forms only

Compose from circles, bars, and squares. No gradients, no ornament a ruler and compass couldn't draw.

The grid is visible

Alignment is the decoration. Hard rules divide the page; the underlying grid is shown, not hidden.

Colour with intent

One signature colour leads each composition. The rest is ink and surface. Colour marks the gear, never fills for filling's sake.
