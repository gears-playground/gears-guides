The Intake Gear

How requests, feedback, and ideas enter the Gears system.

Purpose

Intake is the front door. It captures every signal — feature requests, bug reports, partner asks, internal observations — in one canonical place, with enough structure that downstream gears can act on it.

Input

Raw signal from any source: users, operators, agents, stakeholders.

Output

A normalized intake item, routed to the right downstream gear.
