Intake

How requests, feedback, and ideas enter the Product gear.

Intake isn't a gear — it's a process that happens inside every gear. This page documents Product's intake: the front door through which raw signal becomes candidate product work.

Purpose

Intake is Product's front door. It captures every signal — feature requests, bug reports, partner asks, internal observations — in one canonical place, with enough structure that Product can scope and prioritize it.

Input

Raw signal from any source: users, operators, agents, stakeholders.

Output

A normalized intake item, ready for Product to scope and prioritize.
