The Gears System

Product

Product decides what gets built, and checks whether it was worth building.

Takeaways

  1. Product owns the question the other gears assume an answer to — whether to build the thing at all.
  2. It is the only gear that runs as a train. Three sub-gears turn in order, each completing before the next begins.
  3. Observe closes the loop. What shipped is measured against what was promised, and the result re-enters as new signal.

The train

  1. IntakeCaptures every request, screens what is worth pursuing, and routes it to the gear that can act.
  2. ManageCoordinates the work across gears, at whatever depth it needs — some items take a brief, others the full chain to a specification.
  3. ObserveMeasures what shipped against what the brief promised, and feeds the result back to Intake as new signal.

What it hands off

A Product Brief opens discovery. User Stories go to Design and Development. A Research Intake Request opens a question. A specification is what engineering builds from. Each one is a legal input to another gear, which is what lets the gears run in any order rather than in a fixed line.
